# Customer Voice

Read [the workflow contract](references/operating-contract.md) before execution. For this workflow, also read [knowledge and coaching](references/knowledge-and-coaching.md).

Resolve topic, dates, account/persona scope and relevant trackers. Retrieve matching sessions or disclose a semantic-search sample. Verify verbatim quotes in transcripts/messages and separate customer speech from employee speech.

Return themes with speaker/account/date/source, context, unique-account counts and coverage gaps. Deduplicate recordings. Do not present paraphrases as quotations or absence of retrieved evidence as absence of feedback.

When asked, save the findings with sources to the appropriate document or product resource. Customer quotes for internal analysis are not automatically approved for marketing or outreach.
