# Team Pipeline

Read [the workflow contract](../../references/operating-contract.md) before execution. For this workflow, also read [pipeline and frameworks](../../references/pipeline-and-frameworks.md), [review actions](../../references/review-actions.md).

Resolve the team and whether descendants belong in scope; do not silently widen a personal request. Discover views with that scope and use `groupPipelineView` for stage/owner totals.

Inspect material at-risk deals and framework gaps, linking every coaching suggestion to evidence. Return rep/stage totals, concentration, important risks and concrete manager interventions. Do not assume access to teammate mailboxes. Create requested follow-up work or prepare communications.
