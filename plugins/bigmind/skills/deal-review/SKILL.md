# Deal Review

Read [the workflow contract](../../references/operating-contract.md) before execution. For this workflow, also read [pipeline and frameworks](../../references/pipeline-and-frameworks.md), [review actions](../../references/review-actions.md).

Resolve the deal and current CRM state. Read its stored warnings and applicable framework assessment; investigate material unknowns with deal-linked activity and specific meeting evidence.

Return current stage/amount/date, verified strengths, evidence gaps, risks and the highest-impact next action. Preserve the framework's actual score and coverage; do not add arbitrary percentage adjustments to win probability.

When requested, save supported framework findings, create tasks, prepare a stakeholder email or open the opportunity change card. Do not treat the assessment write as a CRM stage update.
