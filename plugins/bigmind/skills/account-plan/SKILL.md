# Account Plan

Read [the workflow contract](../../references/operating-contract.md) before execution. For this workflow, also read [knowledge and coaching](../../references/knowledge-and-coaching.md), [review actions](../../references/review-actions.md).

Read current account/deal context, contacts, prior plan and customer-stated goals. Use approved product and ICP resources; identify expansion hypotheses only where evidence exists.

Produce goals, relationship map, commercial position, risks, opportunity hypotheses and dated actions. Distinguish agreed owners/deadlines from proposed ones. For a requested saved plan inspect the template and existing document before writing/updating; preserve unrelated content.

Create requested tasks, prepare outreach and review supported opportunity updates. Account description, owner and next-step field writes require a separate exposed tool; do not put them into the opportunity review card.
