# Close Plan

Read [the workflow contract](references/operating-contract.md) before execution. For this workflow, also read [knowledge and coaching](references/knowledge-and-coaching.md), [review actions](references/review-actions.md).

Read the opportunity, customer-stated pain/outcomes, prior proposals and procurement/security/legal commitments. Consult actual internal approval requirements and turnaround times when available.

Draft a business case and work backward from the target signature date into validation, approvals, procurement and signature steps. Mark unconfirmed dates and customer owners as proposed. Calculate ROI only from sourced or explicitly supplied inputs; identify missing inputs instead of inventing them.

Save requested documents using a resolved template, create requested tasks, prepare the champion email and supported opportunity changes through cards. A plan is not mutually agreed until customer evidence establishes agreement.
