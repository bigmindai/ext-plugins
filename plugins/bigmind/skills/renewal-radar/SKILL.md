# Renewal Radar

Read [the workflow contract](../../references/operating-contract.md) before execution. For this workflow, also read [pipeline and frameworks](../../references/pipeline-and-frameworks.md), [review actions](../../references/review-actions.md).

Establish where renewal dates and amounts are stored in the available CRM fields. Resolve the requested window and complete cohort or state coverage limits. Distinguish contract renewal/notice dates from opportunity close dates.

Assess relationship engagement, escalations, stakeholder changes and usage only where supported. Return renewal timing, amount, evidence-backed risk, next action and any expansion signal.

Prepare requested recovery outreach, tasks, saved plans or stage/amount/date cards. Missing renewal opportunities require an actual creation tool or a Bigmind handoff; do not claim the update card creates them.
