# Win Loss Review

Read [the workflow contract](../../references/operating-contract.md) before execution. For this workflow, also read [pipeline and frameworks](../../references/pipeline-and-frameworks.md), [knowledge and coaching](../../references/knowledge-and-coaching.md).

Resolve period, owner scope and actual won/lost definitions. Retrieve the closed cohort with completeness/currency checks; use recorded outcomes rather than assuming a missing opportunity was won. Join each sampled conversation to the right deal.

Compute supported win rates and amounts with denominators. Inspect meaningful wins and losses for explicit reasons, contradictory evidence and unknown causes. Loss-stage distribution and time-in-stage need history.

Return patterns, sample sizes, representative evidence and specific process/coaching actions. Create requested tasks, playbooks, battlecards or coaching resources; do not claim causal effects or calibrated predictive weights from associations.
