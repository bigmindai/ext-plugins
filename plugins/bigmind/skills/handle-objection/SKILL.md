# Handle Objection

Read [the workflow contract](../../references/operating-contract.md) before execution. For this workflow, also read [knowledge and coaching](../../references/knowledge-and-coaching.md), [review actions](../../references/review-actions.md).

Retrieve the exact objection and relevant deal context. Distinguish a stated concern from an inferred negotiating motive. Search approved case studies, product/security documents and competitor resources; verify quoted customer evidence.

Return the underlying concern, a concise talk track, supporting proof and a question that advances the conversation. Acknowledge relevant competitor strengths. Do not invent discounts, compliance guarantees or historical win rates.

Prepare a requested reply using the email card. Keep private cross-customer evidence out of customer-facing copy unless approved for that use. Save a requested reusable response in the appropriate Library resource.
